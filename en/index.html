Good to know

Questions, answered

What to expect from estimates, Pro, and privacy.

Do I need an account to use Cal8?

No. Your profile, food log, favorites, and settings are stored on your iPhone without a user account.

Are calorie values exact?

No. Values are estimates and can vary with recipes, ingredients, and serving sizes. Check the dish details and use your own judgment.

How does AI photo estimation work?

Pro can estimate visible food, portion size, calories, and macros from a photo. You choose the photo and tap Estimate; review the result before saving. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

What does Pro include, and how much does it cost?

Pro includes unlimited meal suggestions, a 7-day meal plan with a grouped shopping list, and about 300 AI assistant messages a month. Photo food estimates are also a Pro feature. Current pricing is shown in the app.

Does Cal8 send any data off my iPhone?

Your profile and meal log stay on your iPhone. If you choose AI or nearby search, the relevant photo, chat, or location data is sent for that feature. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Is Cal8 medical advice?

No. Cal8 is a general meal-planning and tracking app, not medical advice. For health guidance specific to you, speak with a qualified health professional.
