Good to know

A few things to know

Clear answers about calorie estimates, nearby search, and Pro features.

Are the calorie values exact?

They are estimates compiled from multiple sources. Recipes, restaurants, and serving sizes can change the actual value.

Can I set a goal and log meals?

Yes. Set a daily calorie target, save dishes you eat, and review your logged meals and daily progress.

Does nearby search confirm a dish is available?

No. Nearby search helps you find places; it does not confirm that a place serves a particular dish.

What comes with Pro?

Pro features include a 7-day meal plan, a shopping list grouped by shop type, and an AI assistant. The list groups dishes and does not include ingredient data. Current features and pricing are shown in the app.

Is Cal8 medical advice?

No. Cal8 is a general planning tool. For nutrition or health advice specific to you, speak with a qualified health professional.
