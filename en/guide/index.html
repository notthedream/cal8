Cal8

Make meal planning easier

Cal8 brings meal discovery and food logging together. Set an energy goal, browse dishes from different cuisines, and record what you eat throughout the day.

Set a daily goal

Choose a personal energy goal and use it as a planning reference. Menu calorie values are estimates and can vary with recipes and serving sizes.

Explore and log meals

Browse food, drinks, and desserts, search for something you want, and save meals so you can review your day.

Look for nearby places

When you allow location access, the app can search for nearby places. Search results are places and do not guarantee that every listed dish is available there.

Get started

Download Cal8 from the App Store, then set a goal or start exploring dishes. Current features and prices are shown in the app.

Calorie values are estimates for general planning, not medical advice.
